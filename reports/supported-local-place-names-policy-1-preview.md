## 7. Sources used

* `wikipedia:de` — manual list of German city Wikipedia titles (Munich → München, etc.).
* `wikipedia:es` — manual list of Spanish city Wikipedia titles (Córdoba, Málaga, etc.).
* `wikipedia:tr` — manual list of Turkish city Wikipedia titles (İstanbul, İzmir, Şanlıurfa, etc.).
* `wikipedia:ms` — manual list of Malaysian city Wikipedia titles (George Town).
* `geonames:alt+id-wp` — Indonesian `Kota X` form extracted from GeoNames alternatenames for entries where the form is present AND id.wikipedia.org confirms it as the canonical municipal article title.

_All sources are static text; no live translation, no API calls at apply time._
